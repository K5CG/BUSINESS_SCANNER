## 2. 📱 Types of Data Collected and Processed

The application collects and processes the following categories of data:

### A. Scanned Data (Business Cards & Commercial Documents)
- **Scanned Images**: Photographs of business cards and commercial documents taken via the camera or selected from the device photo gallery.
- **Extracted Text (OCR Data)**: First Name, Last Name, Professional Title/Role, Company/Organization Name, Physical Address, Phone Numbers, Email Addresses, Website URLs, VAT Number, and Tax Code.

> 🔒 **ON-DEVICE OCR PROCESSING**:  
> Standard text extraction (OCR) from business cards takes place **100% locally on the user's device** using native machine learning libraries (*Google ML Kit Text Recognition*). Scanned images and extracted text **are NOT sent to external servers** or third-party cloud services during normal local use.

### B. License Management Data
- **Device Identifier (Installation ID)**: A unique non-reversible identifier generated for license activation, trial status, and device limit enforcement.
- **User Email**: Email address associated with license activation or support.
- **License Key**: Alphanumeric key for software activation.

### C. Technical System Data
- Essential technical metrics (OS version, device model). The application **does NOT collect GPS location data, nor advertising tracking data**.

## 4. 🔒 Data Storage and Retention

- **Secure Transmission**: License verification calls to the server use encrypted **HTTPS (TLS 1.3)** protocols.
- **Local Contact Storage**: Scanned contacts and notes reside **exclusively in the local SQLite database on the user's phone**. The user retains full control and can edit or delete data at any time.
- **Retention Period**:
  - Local device data: Stored until manually deleted by the user or until the app is uninstalled.
  - Server license data: Retained for the active license period plus 10 years as required by applicable tax and accounting laws.
